FAQ Services & Info
What sports does NLH Media cover?
NLH Media covers all high school and youth sports including football, basketball, baseball, soccer, track, volleyball, and more. If your team plays, TJ can film it.
How long does it take to receive my highlight reel?
Most highlight reels and game film packages are delivered within 3–5 business days after the event. Rush delivery is available upon request.
Do you work with individual athletes or only schools?
Both. NLH Media works directly with school programs, coaching staffs, and individual athletes who need recruiting content or personal highlight packages.
What areas does NLH Media serve?
NLH Media is based in the Florida Panhandle and serves schools and athletes across the Gulf Coast region. Travel is available for select events.
